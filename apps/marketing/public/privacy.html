Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 July 2026

Jefe ("Jefe", "we", "us") is an AI eCommerce manager for Shopify stores, operated by Quiver Solutions Limited (company no. 16961611), registered at 27 Old Gloucester Street, London WC1N 3AX. This policy explains what data we access, why, how we protect it, and your rights.

1. Our two roles

As a processor (on the merchant's behalf): when Jefe processes data about a merchant's end customers (e.g. names, emails, addresses on orders), the merchant is the data controller and Jefe is the processor, acting on the merchant's instructions under our Data Processing Agreement.

As a controller: for the merchant's own account and usage data (the person who installs and uses Jefe), we act as controller.

2. What we access and collect

From your Shopify store (via Shopify's API, with your authorisation, and Shopify webhooks): orders and order line items, refunds; products, variants, inventory levels, locations; customer identities associated with orders — which may include name, email, address, and phone ("protected customer data"); and store configuration and policies.

About your use of Jefe: account identifiers, the memory you confirm and correct, settings, and technical logs (identifiers and metadata, not customer data content).

We request only the Shopify scopes needed to provide the service.

3. Why we process it

To provide the service: to build your Merchant Memory (a structured, correctable understanding of your business) and to generate insights, goals, and recommendations. Legal basis: performance of our contract with you and our legitimate interests in operating and improving the service.

4. How we process it

Deterministic code computes reliable commerce facts. A large language model interprets bounded evidence into memory, questions, and recommendations; our application validates and stores the result, and your corrections outrank the model. Jefe is advisory — it recommends; you decide. It does not write to your Shopify store or other systems without your explicit approval through a controlled, reversible action.

We do not sell your data. We use data from your store to operate Jefe and to develop and improve the service — including refining the structured understanding and models that power it. Where we use data beyond providing your own store's functionality, we use aggregated or de-identified data wherever possible, and handle personal data in line with applicable law and Shopify's requirements. 7. Retention & deletion

We retain your Merchant Memory and supporting data for the duration of the service (while Jefe is installed). We honour Shopify's mandatory privacy webhooks: customers/redact (erase a specific customer's data and delete their identity record), customers/data_request (assemble data held about a customer), and shop/redact (delete a shop's data on uninstall). On uninstall, your shop-scoped data is hard-deleted within approximately 48 hours (via shop/redact), except where we must retain it for legal reasons. Encrypted database backups age out separately over our backup-retention window of [BACKUP WINDOW — confirm from Neon console, commonly ~7 days].

8. Security

Encryption at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS/HTTPS); access controls limiting staff access to protected data; access logging; an incident-response process; and separation of test and production data.

9. Your rights

Merchants can inspect and correct their Merchant Memory in-product at any time, and can request access, correction, or deletion of their account data. End customers should exercise their rights (access/erasure) with the merchant (the controller); we support the merchant via the Shopify webhooks above. To exercise rights or ask questions: hola@mynamejefe.com.

10. International transfers

Data may be processed in the UK, the European Union, and the United States, depending on our infrastructure providers' regions [confirm Neon + Railway console regions]; our LLM sub-processor (Google Gemini) runs on Google's global infrastructure. Where data leaves the UK/EEA, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses.
