## Persona framework (the A/B variables)

Jefe's name is a wink (22 Jump Street — "my name's Jefe"), and the brand voice is deliberately flexible. We spin up distinct personalities, A/B them from the brand accounts, keep what converts, and let the personality *grow up* on the data — like raising a kid. Starting set:

1. **El Jefe — the boss / expert.** Assured, operator-to-operator, credible, no fluff. "I've seen a thousand stores; here's the move."
2. **Cheeky Jefe — the upstart.** Tongue-in-cheek, self-aware, funny; roasts dashboards and chatbots. The "my name's Jefe" energy.
3. **Baby Jefe — the newborn.** Just born, learning your store, needs founding partners to raise him (the site's angle). Endearing, underdog, community-building.
4. **Straight-talker — the contrarian.** Sharp thesis takes on why most "AI for Shopify" is noise; earns attention with a point of view.

Each post idea gets rendered in ≥2 personas → posted to brand accounts → ranked by engagement/click/follow → winners inform the next batch. Personality **evolves** on results, and can **split by market** as we expand.

## Content pillars (rotate)

1. **Build-in-public** — what we shipped/learned; a founder building an AI eCommerce manager in the open.
2. **The thesis** — Merchant Memory: why an AI that *understands* your store beats another dashboard/chatbot; the advisory→autonomy ramp.
3. **Design-partner journey** — *aha* moments + wins (consented only).
4. **Operator value** — a concrete weekly decision a Shopify operator makes, and how Jefe handles it (reorder, budget shift, price match, returns).
5. **Engagement** — reply to DTC founders/operators discussing the pains Jefe solves.

**Cadence (ramp up):** X 3–5×/week + a daily engagement window; LinkedIn 2–3×/week. **Voice guardrail:** never overclaim — present tense is advisory; actioning is what Jefe *earns*.
