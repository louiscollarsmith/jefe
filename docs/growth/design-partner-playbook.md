# Design Partner Playbook — recruiting the first 10 (stage 0→10)

> **Owner:** Jefe chat 6 · **Last updated:** 2026-07-29 · Companion to [commercial-state.md](commercial-state.md) (ICP §2, activation §3) + [growth-strategy.md](growth-strategy.md)

## The finding that sets the priority

The `apps/growth` tracker runs against the live waitlist and reports **~1 signup**. The machine (tracker, ICP triage, email sender, positioning) is built; the funnel is empty. So the constraint at 0→10 is **acquisition of the first design partners**, and the motion is founder-led, personal, and hand-to-hand — not automation. This playbook is the fuel.

Target math: **~40–60 hand-picked ICP prospects → ~10 activated design partners.**

## Who to reach (ICP restated)

Established DTC Shopify brand, **single store** (international sales fine), ~$1–20M GMV, hands-on founder/eComm-manager operator, no analyst, real catalog + repeat behaviour. Prioritise by reachability:

1. **Warm intros** (highest conversion) — founders you know, or one hop away.
2. **DTC founder communities** — the Slacks/Discords/forums where $1–20M operators actually hang out.
3. **E-commerce agencies** — each manages several ICP stores = leverage per relationship.
4. **Your own audience** — the mynamejefe.com brand/video; anyone already engaging.

## Sourcing method (build the target list)

1. Pull 40–60 names across the four buckets above; for each capture: brand, ~GMV proxy (catalog depth / traffic / team size), store URL, contact, source, warmth (warm/cold), why-them.
2. Score against the §2 qualification checklist — chase warm + on-ICP first.
3. Record it somewhere trackable (see **Track it** below).

## Outreach — personal 1:1, from Matt's inbox (NOT bulk)

**Rule:** 0→10 outreach is personal and low-volume, sent from Matt's own inbox — no campaign tooling, no bulk sends (protects deliverability and it converts better). Every message names a specific reason for *that* brand.

**Template A — warm / community**
> Subject: building an AI eComm manager — want your brain
>
> Hey [name] — I'm building **Jefe**, an AI eCommerce manager for Shopify brands. It learns how your store actually works and tells you the next move, with the receipts behind it. I'm hand-picking ~10 founding design partners to teach it their store before we open up — **free during the program**, founding-partner pricing after. You + [brand] came to mind because [specific reason]. Worth a quick 15 min? — Matt

**Template B — direct, founder-to-founder**
> Subject: [brand] + Jefe (founding design partner?)
>
> Hi [name] — founder to founder: I'm building Jefe, an AI manager that learns how a Shopify store runs and recommends the next move (you approve; over time it earns the right to just handle the routine stuff). Looking for a few founding partners running real $[X]M brands to shape it. Free while in the program. Could I show you what it already knows about a store like [brand] on a 15-min call?

**Template C — agency**
> Subject: early access to Jefe for a couple of your brands
>
> Hey [name] — you run [agency] and touch a bunch of Shopify brands. I'm building Jefe (AI eCommerce manager — learns each store, recommends the next move). Want to put it on one or two of your brands as a founding design partner? Free in-program, and I'd love an operator's eye on it.

*Voice check:* Jefe is a character you're raising ("my name is Jefe" / "help Jefe / teach him your store"). Warm, specific, never salesy.

## The qualifying call (~15 min)

1. **Qualify** (2 min): confirm the ICP — GMV band, single Shopify store, they're the hands-on operator, and the pain ("can't keep the whole business in my head").
2. **Show, don't tell** (5 min): if they'll connect (or on a demo store), show what Jefe already *knows* about the business — aim for the **"yes, that's exactly how my business works"** moment. That's activation.
3. **The ramp** (2 min): advisory today → it earns autonomy per action type as they trust it. Merchant is always in control.
4. **The ask** (3 min): become a founding design partner — connect Shopify, give feedback, **free during the program + a founding-partner rate** after. Agree next step (onboard now or book it).

**Activation target** (from commercial-state §3): memory confirmed accurate **and** ≥1 recommendation accepted. Track who hits it.
