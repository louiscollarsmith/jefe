# Design-Partner Case Study — capture kit

> **Owner:** Jefe chat 6 · **Last updated:** 2026-07-29 · Companion to [design-partner-playbook.md](design-partner-playbook.md)
>
> **Why now:** the first 3–4 design partners go live this week. Each one is a reusable proof asset — the fuel every channel needs (App Store reviews, LinkedIn/X posts, outbound, the website). **Proof before amplification.** Capture it *as it happens*, not months later.

## Consent first (hard rule)

Never publish a merchant's name, brand, numbers, or quote without their **explicit OK**. On the design-partner call, ask: *"If Jefe delivers, are you open to us sharing your story?"* Default to anonymised (\"a $Xm apparel brand\") until they say yes to attribution. Same bar the product side holds on anything outbound.

## What to capture, and when

| Moment | Capture | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| **Onboarding (day 0)** | The *before*: their words on the pain — "I can't keep the whole business in my head." Baseline: GMV band, catalog size, team, what they're worried about. | The "before" half of before/after. |
| **Activation (the aha)** | The moment they see Merchant Memory and say **"yes, that's exactly how my business works."** Screenshot (with permission) + their reaction verbatim. | This IS the story. It's the north-star moment made concrete. |
| **First win (week 1–2)** | The first recommendation they acted on + what happened. | Turns "it understands me" into "it made me money / saved me time." |
| **Week 2–4** | Any metric movement + a pull quote. | Credibility. Optional; the aha + first win carry it alone. |

## The template (fill one per partner)

```
Partner:        [Name, role] — [Brand] ([category], ~$Xm GMV)   [attributed? y/n]
Before:         [their words on the pain, verbatim if possible]
The aha:        [what Jefe surfaced that made them say "that's my business"]
First win:      [recommendation acted on → outcome]
Metric(s):      [optional, only if real and consented]
Quote:          "[short, punchy, real]" — [Name, Brand]
Consent:        [anonymised | attributed | not for external use]
```

## Turn one capture into many assets

From a single filled template, produce (each needs the consent level to match):
- **One-line proof** — for the website / a deck: *"[Brand] connected Jefe and within a day said 'that's exactly how my business works.'"*
- **App Store review ask** — nudge the activated partner to leave a review at the aha moment (highest-intent moment).
- **LinkedIn/X post** — the design-partner-journey story (build-in-public), drafted by the content engine → your approval → post.
- **Outbound proof** — a line to drop into the [outreach templates](design-partner-playbook.md) for similar brands: *"just helped [similar brand] with X."*
- **Full case study** — the whole template, lightly edited, for the site once we have a few.

## Process

1. Keep one file per partner (start under `docs/growth/case-studies/` or a shared doc — your call).
2. chat 6 drafts the assets from each filled template; **you approve before anything goes public.**
3. Feed the best lines back into the website, App Store listing, and the content engine.
